GPS Viewer

Opens the live GPS page served by the ESP32. Your phone or computer must be on the same Wi-Fi as the ESP32.

On your Wi-Fi

Open GPS Viewer
http://gps.local

By IP address

Use this if gps.local doesn't open (common on Android). The IP is printed on the ESP32's USB serial at boot, or listed as gps in your router's device list. This page remembers it.

No Wi-Fi around

Open on the ESP32-GPS hotspot
Join Wi-Fi ESP32-GPS, password gps12345, then tap above

Coordinates, satellites and track all work. The map stays blank because the hotspot has no internet.

Tips

  • Bookmark this page, or add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen).
  • Board LED: blinking red = no GPS data, blinking amber = searching for satellites, solid green = position fix.
  • The first fix needs open sky: 1 to 5 minutes at a window or outside.
